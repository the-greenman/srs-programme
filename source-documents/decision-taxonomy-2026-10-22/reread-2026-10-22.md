Semantic records: only rfc-decisions, RFC requirements, invariants and contract-free ADRs (0b1c25b1, scoped). Both stand only because each holds in its own domain.
2. **Made local, written global:** ff946eb1 (editor comment UX), 2800bea0 (Agent memory clusters), 2637af0a, f58db200 (made for the practice's labels; wording is universal: confirm whether ROOT).
3. **Application local, principle global:** 46bb7a8c (the OpenAPI document is a PC application; "we build APIs from the spec, the spec is the source of truth" holds in SR too) and e745e9b2 (loopback/ZeroTier is PC; "tooling that acts with the owner's authority is security-aware" is broader). Lift the principle to the scope it really has; keep the application in its domain.
4. **Deliberately global, and says so:** d529294a ("for the central group and for every working group"), 559bfb9e (and it scopes one-way-per-goal to spec and code, not repositories). The good form.
5. PC has no group (a8bc55ef proposes one), so all PC decisions land in the root log. A domain without a group has no log of its own.
