# Decision clusters, first pass (2026-10-22)

**Corpus.** 101 decisions: the 54 srs `rfc-decision` records (2026-07-29 to 2026-10-20) and the 47 srs-programme `decision` records since 2026-10-12. The 40 earlier programme decisions were re-read on 2026-10-11 (clerk `docs/decision-log.md`) and are not yet in this pass.

**Method.** No kinds are named. Each clustering groups decisions by one property. Cluster labels say what the members share, not what they are called. Assignments are one reader's first reading; disagreements are the data.

Prefixes: `s:` srs, `p:` srs-programme.

---

## The properties

Ten properties every decision has, each varying from decision to decision. The clusterings below use the first seven.

| # | Property | Question it answers | Values seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Object | What does it govern? | how a thing is represented; where a thing belongs; how things change over time; whether a thing exists; who may act; how work flows; how decisions are made; which way we lean |
| 2 | Form | What is the shape of the statement? | X is Y; every X must Y; X over Y unless Z; X only once C; G may X; X covers A not B; we work as if X; X is removed until T; A lands with B; do this now |
| 3 | Time | How long does it hold? | standing; until a named phase; deferred until a trigger; provisional, to test; one case |
| 4 | Lineage | How does it relate to other decisions? | founds an instrument; applies one; refines or corrects one; carves an exception; supersedes in part; stands alone |
| 5 | Scope | How far does it reach, and does it say where it stops? | whole system; a plane or layer; one construct; one operation; one domain or group; one tool; one case |
| 6 | Grounding | What justifies it? | a principle or axis; usage evidence; an incident; the owner's word; borrowed from a sibling practice |
| 7 | Review shape | What would show it is wrong? | a counterexample appears; a consumer or trigger arrives; a phase flips; a measure crosses a line; it is never used (ritual); it is gamed |
| 8 | Discretion | Were there legitimate alternatives? | yes (a decision); no (a derivation) |
| 9 | Parts | One claim, or an instrument with parts? | single; instrument (several parts, one purpose); bundle (several parts, several purposes) |
| 10 | Layer | Which plane does it speak on? | spec meaning, expression or operation (srs); governance, tool or implementation (programme) |

| **An instrument with parts** | s:cce3c00e grid; s:9ee14517 layer rules; s:c8704763 reference strengths; s:e99a9437 doors; s:7caca3a1 modes; s:5f8204bc retirement per layer; p:e3505ded choice, ruling, escalation | many parts, one purpose: a vocabulary later decisions cite |
| **A bundle** | p:05c3ddcb (six); p:926c059f (ten); p:3b910d90; p:0a87ebf6; s:1967b8eb (1)(2)(3); s:4f1e12e5 (a list of removals) | many parts, several purposes |

**What it shows.**
- Form does not line up with object. "Every X must Y" governs representation (s:8948e43f), change (s:628cf6c4) and decision-making (p:bbb23e36) alike. So kind cannot be one property: form and object are separate axes.
- **Instruments and bundles look alike but are not.** An instrument has many parts that serve one purpose and are cited together later; it passes "one thing" at the level of its purpose. A bundle has parts that serve different purposes, and each is cited separately. The "one thing" test needs that distinction.
- The "unless" and the belief live outside srs's decision records (in the grid's preferences and boundary clauses). The programme writes them into the records themselves. That is a structural difference between the two practices, not a gap in either.

- One-case decisions exist in srs too (86f49083 is a one-off release train). Its general form was ruled two months later, in the other log (p:a8e833f1). See clustering 6.

**What it shows.**
- **The srs decisions that stand alone predate the grid.** 17 of 54 cite no earlier decision, cell or axis. 16 of them were made on or before 2026-08-21, the day the grid was adopted; most date from 07-29 to 08-01 and are short, one sentence, with no cell. **What it shows.**

- srs's negative scope ("Does not change rules 1, 2, 3, 5 or 6. Does not alter Invariant 28.") is the property that keeps a domain decision from becoming global. The programme has nowhere to put it.

| Attribution | 16b20c56: optional, one shape, never authority; d5524758: `createdBy` stamped from the session | 21ee9352: every agent comment names its actor | The same principle (stated over assumed) at two scopes: data model and PR practice. |
| What counts as authority for a group's merges? | e99a9437: door 1 executes a recorded ruling | 2637af0a (editor group): an affirmed remedy counts; 0b1c25b1 (Third-party ready): only spec records and contract-free ADRs | Different on purpose. Each holds only in its own domain. |

## Properties that do not discriminate

- **srs `governing_values`.** shared-coherence is on 45 of 54 and semantic-integrity on 42. A property nearly every decision shares tells you nothing about any one of them, which is the same failure as the programme's `policy` label (24 of 47). Low spread is a sign that a property needs splitting or dropping.
- **srs `project_phase`.** It is "formation" on all 53 that carry it. It will start to discriminate only after the first phase change.

## Candidates to watch (not kinds yet)

These are where clusters from different properties coincide. If they keep coinciding as more decisions arrive, they are kinds.

1. **Representation × "X is Y" × counterexample review × construct scope**: the srs design core. It coincides strongly.
2. **Who may act × permission or prohibition form × domain or group scope**: almost all programme. It coincides strongly, and it sits worst in the current seven kinds.
3. **Change over time × "every X must Y" or sequence form × phase review**.
4. **Existence × removed-until-trigger form × deferred time × trigger review**: srs only.
5. **Instrument × founds lineage × system scope × ritual-guard review**: the grid, the layer rules, the doors, the modes, choice/ruling/escalation.
6. **Orientation × belief or comparative form × until a phase**: the programme's premises and leans; in srs, the grid itself.
