# The three axes, by trigram

SemanticOps' local reference for how a three-axis structure is laid out. **The three-axis structure is trigram based** (owner, 2026-10-23). It applies to the T3 stance lines, a domain's three axes, and decisions placed on them. The SRS compass is zodiac based (owner, 2026-10-23); it is placed on these lines only through the alignment below, as a translation.

## Two synthetic systems, two maps

The trigram and the zodiac are **different synthetic systems that map the same space** (owner, 2026-10-23). They are related, but they are different maps, built from different rubrics.

- **The trigram (three axes) is the default for a domain.** It is built directly from tensions, one per line.
- **The zodiac (the SRS compass) makes gaps easier to find.** It has twelve cells and six axes on a fixed grid, so an empty cell or axis is a question the domain has not asked.
- **Moving between them is a translation.** It goes through the alignment below, and is stated whenever it is used.

## The rule

1. **A trigram is three lines, read from the bottom.**
   - Line 1 is **Earth**, line 2 is **Human**, line 3 is **Heaven**: the Three Powers.
   - A hexagram is two trigrams: lines 1 to 3 below, lines 4 to 6 above. Lines 4, 5 and 6 are Earth, Human and Heaven of the upper trigram.
2. **Line k is the zodiac axis that begins at sign k.**
   - Line 1 is Aries ↔ Libra, line 2 Taurus ↔ Scorpio, and so on to line 6, Virgo ↔ Pisces.
   - Each line's Three Powers position corresponds to a **modality**: Earth is Cardinal, Human is Fixed, Heaven is Mutable.
3. **The two trigrams pair line by line through modality.** Lines 1 and 4 are Cardinal, 2 and 5 are Fixed, 3 and 6 are Mutable. So "the same line" in both trigrams means the same modality.
4. **The line's position, not its name, carries the meaning.** Each instrument names its lines in its own words. The table below is the one place where they meet.

## Polarity

- **Odd lines are yang positions, even lines are yin positions.** Classic element polarity agrees: the axes on lines 1, 3 and 5 join Fire and Air signs (yang elements), and the axes on lines 2, 4 and 6 join Earth and Water signs (yin elements). Opposite signs always share an element polarity, so element polarity classifies the line, not the pole within it. This system does not use element polarity for the signs themselves (next point).
- **On the zodiac, each sign's polarity is set by rule** (owner, 2026-10-23). Yin and yang alternate around the signs, starting with Aries as yang, except at 6/7 (Virgo → Libra) and 12/1 (Pisces → Aries), where they do not alternate.

  | ♈ | ♉ | ♊ | ♋ | ♌ | ♍ | ♎ | ♏ | ♐ | ♑ | ♒ | ♓ |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | yang | yin | yang | yin | yang | yin | yin | yang | yin | yang | yin | yang |

  - So the yang pole is the first sign on axes 1-7, 3-9 and 5-11, and the second sign on 2-8, 4-10 and 6-12.
  - The ratified SRS compass matches on all twelve signs: Practical expression (♈), Evolution (♏), Shared coherence (♊), Office (♑), Renewal (♌), Portability (♓).
  - The synthetic practice maps the domain first: which concept sits in which cell. Polarity then follows from the rule.
  - Two cross-checks follow from applying the rule correctly. Every element holds both poles, split 2–1. Every level (Individual, Relational, Systemic) splits 2–2. A failure means a concept is on the wrong cell. On 2026-10-23, Shared coherence was first put on ♐ Governance, which gave Fire 3–0. It belongs on ♊ Description ("one name over many").
- **On the trigram, which pole of a line is yang is assigned per domain.**
  - A narrower domain may flip it (decision cdd272a3).
  - The original note's yang column takes the first sign of each axis (Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo). That is not this system's rule: see the zodiac rule above.
  - A compass pole translated onto a trigram line can read differently, because they are two maps. Portability (♓) is yang by the zodiac rule, but on trigram line 3 a capability that travels aligns with other actors, which reads as yin.
  - Use the owner's yang attributes instead (clerk#159): thing over relation, movement over stillness, outward over inward, assertive over reflective, forward looking over retrospective.
- **Yang is not the preferred pole.** Preference is a lean, recorded separately.

## Using it

- **Placing a decision:** rate each line in its own question (the stance column), with the boundary stated first. The code is the three letters in line order: line 1, then line 2, then line 3.
- **Placing a domain's three axes:** use the trigram, and give each axis the line whose Three Powers question it answers. Then translate onto the compass to look for gaps. Line 1: where the change lands, on the world or on the system itself. Line 2: whether to keep or transform. Line 3: whether to hold the boundary or open it. Then test the pairs and the cube (clerk#159, steps 3 and 4).
- **Reading a decision against its domain (context × action):** pair line k with line k. Both now use the same line order, so line k means the same Three Powers position in each.
